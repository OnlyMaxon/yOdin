# Delete your yOdin account

**App:** yOdin (`app.yodin`)
**Developer:** Jalil Orujli, Chmielna 69, Warsaw, Poland
**Contact:** [hello@onlymaxon.com](mailto:hello@onlymaxon.com)

This page explains how to delete your yOdin account and what happens to your
data. See also the [privacy policy](/yOdin/privacy-policy/).

---

## Delete it yourself, in the app

1. Open **yOdin** and sign in.
2. Go to the **Profile** tab.
3. Open **Settings** (the menu button in the top corner).
4. Tap **Delete account**.
5. Read what will be removed, enter your password to confirm, and tap **Delete
   permanently**.

Deletion starts immediately. There is no waiting period and no way to undo it,
so make sure you have saved anything you want to keep before you confirm.

## If you no longer have the app

Email **[hello@onlymaxon.com](mailto:hello@onlymaxon.com)** from the address you
registered with, asking us to delete your account. We reply within 30 days, and
usually much sooner. We may ask you to confirm from that address if the request
arrives from somewhere else — otherwise anyone could delete your account by
writing to us.

---

## What is deleted

Everything below is removed permanently:

| Data | Notes |
|---|---|
| Your account and sign-in credentials | You can no longer sign in |
| Profile — name, @handle, bio, nationality, country, languages, profile photo | — |
| Your posts and your discussions | Including replies and comments other people left under them |
| Photos and videos you uploaded | Removed from file storage |
| Likes, saved items, event sign-ups | — |
| Who you follow, and your place in other people's follower lists | — |
| Notifications addressed to you | — |
| Your block list | — |

## What is kept, and why

| Data | Kept for | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Replies and comments you left under **other people's** posts and discussions, with your name, photo, nationality and country removed | Indefinitely, anonymised | Deleting them would tear holes in other people's conversations and remove answers other readers rely on. They no longer identify you |
| Your @handle | Indefinitely, reserved | So nobody can register your old handle and inherit mentions of you |
| Reports you filed about other people's content | 12 months after they are resolved | So moderation decisions can be reviewed |
| A one-way keyed hash of your email address — **only if your account had moderation strikes or bans** | 12 months | To stop someone who was banned from immediately registering again. It cannot be turned back into your email address, and it is not kept for accounts with a clean record |
| Copies in encrypted backups | Up to 30 days | Backups expire on their own schedule |

Nothing in the "kept" column is used to contact you or to build a profile of
you, and none of it is shared with anyone.

---

## Deleting some data without deleting your account

You do not have to delete your whole account to remove things:

- **A post or discussion you wrote** — open it, then use the delete action.
- **Profile details** — Profile → Settings → Edit profile lets you change or
  clear your photo, bio, and other fields.

To request removal of a specific reply or comment that stayed behind after an
account deletion, write to [hello@onlymaxon.com](mailto:hello@onlymaxon.com).
